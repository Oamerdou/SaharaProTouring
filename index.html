The full spectrum of Moroccan music

Seven traditions, from the Atlas and the desert to the cities. Each one is performed by artists who carry it in their everyday lives.

Gnawa

Spiritual trance music built on the guembri, iron castanets and call-and-response chants.

Amazigh

Songs and rhythms of the Amazigh (Berber) communities of the Atlas and the south.

Saharan

Music from the desert south, shaped by nomadic traditions and open landscapes.

Desert Blues

Hypnotic guitar-led grooves with roots in Saharan melody and rhythm.

Chaabi

Popular music of Moroccan cities, made for celebration and shared singing.

Aita

Powerful poetic songs from the Moroccan countryside, carried by strong voices.

Baldi

Traditional, rooted sounds of the Moroccan heartland.
